# Preaching

**Resolved perspective:** Pastoral teacher speaking to the listener, with scripture quoted in context.

**Quality contract:** Observation, interpretation, application. Each claim about the passage needs textual support or a stated interpretive boundary.

**Opening and outline:** Read/name the text, establish first-audience context, trace the argument, then make one pastoral application.

**Avoid:** Motivational speech wearing biblical language, proof-texting, invented historical setting, denominational overreach, and hype altar-call rhetoric.
