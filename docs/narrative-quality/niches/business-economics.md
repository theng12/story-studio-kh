# Business Economics

**Formula role:** The Economics of… operating-model case studies. Topic Packs cover modern businesses and ancient civilizations.

**Resolved perspective:** Third-person documentary.

**Opening policy:** Begin with one transaction, asset, queue, invoice, capacity limit, or cash problem. Never “So you want to own…,” “Imagine owning…,” dictionary definitions, motivational entrepreneurship language, or earnings bait in the narration.

**Outline contract:** Market assumptions, customer promise, startup capital, revenue equation, fixed costs, variable costs, people and throughput, cash conversion/break-even, failure/scale constraint, ownership verdict. Every material number is tagged sourced fact, representative assumption, or range.

**Quality guardrails:** Separate revenue, gross profit, operating profit, owner pay, cash flow, and return. No universal profitability claims, fake companies, fake regulations, or business-coach moralizing. Explain a mechanism before making a conclusion.

**Future formula work:** Preserve the existing ten-chapter arc in Guided mode and add a claim ledger to the outline. Use the Annotation Business Board thumbnail recipe independently of prose quality rules.
