# Prayer

**Resolved perspective:** Section-scoped formula policy: pastoral address to listener in reflection/blessing; direct address to God in the prayer body.

**Quality contract:** Sincere prayer with one anchored scripture reference, deliberate repetition, and a clear settle line. The perspective transition must be explicit and never accidental.

**Opening and outline:** Reflection/invitation, petition and promise, declaration/blessing. Preserve the existing recency guard for scripture references.

**Avoid:** Performance language, commercial CTA energy, denomination-specific claims, generic therapy language, and accidental mixing of listener and God address inside one section.
