# Philosophy

**Resolved perspective:** First-person conversational guide with occasional inclusive “we”; no forced viewer roleplay.

**Quality contract:** One thinker or school meets one real human problem. Primary-source accuracy matters more than a tidy lesson.

**Opening and outline:** Begin with a concrete dilemma or short source passage, establish context, interpret it, test its limits, and end with one genuine question.

**Avoid:** Quote laundering, life-hack Stoicism, fake profundity, “the ancients knew the secret,” and an answer disguised as an open question.
