# Devotional

**Resolved perspective:** Pastoral teacher speaking to the listener; scripture is quoted or paraphrased with its reference.

**Quality contract:** One passage, two or three observations, one practical takeaway. The text leads; the application follows.

**Opening and outline:** Speak the passage early, give its immediate setting, explain one phrase at a time, then offer a modest daily practice.

**Avoid:** Generic encouragement detached from the passage, invented biblical context, sermon-like hype, and a forced comment CTA.
