# Quiet Anime Poetry — approved visual direction

Owner selected the Qwen Image 2.1 Turbo samples on 2026-10-22 and requested a reusable art-style pack using Qwen-generated artwork for its preview. Qwen is the preferred generation choice, not a dependency or routing field in the style.

## Style bible

Cinematic hand-drawn anime illustration with delicate, confident ink contours, natural adult proportions and restrained clean cel shading. Combine clearly drawn figures and simple readable silhouettes with softly painted, spatially convincing environments. Use smooth sky, glass and light gradients, a pristine digital finish and carefully controlled environmental detail; avoid grain and distressed surface texture. Build colour around muted teal, deep blue and forest green, balanced by selective amber, dusty peach or cream highlights. Light is soft and directional, with luminous windows, evening skies or subtle rim light; keep shadow shapes rich but legible. Convey emotion through understated posture, meaningful spatial relationships, everyday objects and the surrounding atmosphere. Choose scene-specific visual metaphors for the underlying feeling rather than illustrating every spoken sentence literally. Compose original, elegant frames with layered depth, asymmetrical balance and uncluttered breathing room for separately rendered captions when needed. Preserve quiet intimacy, contemplation and emotional ambiguity without theatrical expressions. Adapt time of day, setting, cast and palette balance to each scene instead of repeating a fixed motif. Artwork contains no text; captions are rendered separately.

The generated samples use original scenes and prompts. The reusable style does not prescribe a window, empty chair, leaf, named person, channel or poem.

The pack preview will be an original landscape Qwen image made from this rendering direction. Portrait samples remain supplementary examples. No existing channel, production preset, job or routine is changed by authoring this pack.
