This is the immutable provenance ledger for preview bytes that may be copied
into a Free Starter Channel Kit source pack. `migrated-approved-built-in`
means the checked-in Story Studio built-in preview is the approved migration
source. `publisher-created-tool-generated-original` means an original raster
was created by Story Studio's publisher with the named tool under recorded
owner authorization; it is not an owner-supplied file.
Each approved record's `ownerId` is the exact legacy Style or Recipe
declaration authorized to select those bytes.

Portrait Block Right retain their publisher-created tool-generated index-zero
originals. The appended gallery variants are separately ledgered
owner-supplied saved-output derivatives: their source images remain read-only,
episode/franchise wording is replaced with generic catalog sample wording, and
the output bytes below bind the added recipe previews.
