# Explainer / Listicle Voice

**Resolved perspective:** Third-person conversational explainer.

**Quality contract:** Independent topics are allowed, but they must not feel like identical mini-videos. Rotate opening modes and use a single episode-level promise rather than ten repeated click hooks.

**Opening and outline:** Topic one earns the click. Later topics begin with evidence, a contrast, an object, a consequence, or a question in rotation. Every topic records claim, evidence, explanation, and payoff.

**Avoid:** Hook-question in every section, stock micro-payoffs, “mind-blowing,” fake surprise, and hard-cut filler. This is the niche most in need of a hook-variety ledger.
