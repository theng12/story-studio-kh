# POV: Your Life As…

**Formula role:** Second-person documentary life paths through a real institution, role, or rank ladder. Topic Packs cover law enforcement, criminal hierarchies, and historical occupations.

**Resolved perspective:** Second-person documentary life-path, locked by the formula. This is not the existing “second immersive” role-play mode.

**Opening policy:** Begin at the first institutional threshold: recruitment, entrance requirement, obligation, or first material compromise. Use “you” economically and concretely. Ban “Imagine this,” “you find yourself,” video-game-stat language, and empty cinematic roleplay.

**Outline contract:** Entry conditions, training, ordinary duty, each meaningful status tier, incentives, tools, authority, risks, moral pressure, exit/downfall/retirement. Each tier must materially change the viewer's choices rather than merely raise a rank number.

**Quality guardrails:** Do not assert inner thoughts as fact. Separate documented practice from a representative reconstruction; distinguish place, era, and institution. Avoid glamorising criminal roles or treating violence as spectacle.

**Future formula work:** Add `perspectivePolicy: second-documentary` and a formula-owned 7–9-stage ladder. This prevents Continuity Chain's first-person default from contradicting the selected formula.
