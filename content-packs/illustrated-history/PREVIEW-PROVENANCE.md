## How the previews are produced

Precedent and rules: `docs/VISUAL_STYLE_CONTENT_PACK_FORMAT.md` and
`content-packs/gory-cartoon-history/PREVIEW-PROVENANCE.md`. Previews come from
owner-supplied originals or a Studio Hub production-preview handoff, under an explicit
one-pass owner authorization that names the image model and a credit ceiling. The route
used by the earlier collections is GenStudio text-to-image (`gpt-image-2`) via the Story
Studio Image Pool inside a running app, 16:9, one variant per prompt.

Proposed prompts for one style preview and three varied thumbnail samples are prepared in
`_orchestra/illustrated-sleep-history-20261021/IMAGE-PROMPTS.md`.

## What must be recorded when the bytes arrive

For every retained file, in the form the sleep-history, Lost Voyage, Royal History and
Gory Cartoon records use: the source run id and job id, the original digest and byte count,
the exact conversion applied (whole-image `resize(1672x941, cover, lanczos3)` then WebP
quality 90 effort 6) with paired-encode proof, and the accepted digest, byte count,
dimensions and decode result. Each file must be reviewed at card size for spelling,
legibility, absence of logos/watermarks/UI, and absence of any copied composition.
