## The harness (new capability)
The app was run for real and driven over the Chrome DevTools Protocol, so geometry is measured in a
live renderer rather than reasoned from source:

1. `npx nuxt dev --port 3000` (the app's dev frontend).
2. `electron . --user-data-dir=/tmp/ssk-qa-data --remote-debugging-port=9333` with
   `STORY_STUDIO_DATA_DIR=/tmp/ssk-qa-data` — a **disposable Library**, so the owner's running
   installed app and its real projects are never touched (verified: the installed 1.4.213 app kept
   running, untouched, through the whole pass).
3. A small CDP driver (`/tmp/qa-cdp.mjs`): sets the viewport (`Emulation.setDeviceMetricsOverride`),
   navigates, measures every element's rect and scroll width, and captures a PNG.
4. Fixtures seeded through the app's own API — one project with an intentionally over-long name
   ("QA Long Name Project — Visual Checks 2026") and two episodes with immoderately long titles.
5. The overflow probe **walks each candidate's ancestors for `overflow-x: auto|scroll`** before
   calling anything unreachable, so a deliberate horizontal scroller is not reported as a defect.

Screenshots preserved under `docs/handoffs/deepseek-reports/qa/` (11 routes × 2 viewports).

## Findings — exact failing consumers (F-15, named overflow surfaces)

### F-15a — Scene Plan: the "Avoided imagery" control is unreachable at 1440×900
- **Element:** `label.form-group` inside `div.scene-creative-controls`, within
  `div.scene-setting-card.scene-creative-card`.
- **Geometry:** rect `1313..1533` at a 1440px viewport — **93px past the right edge**, and 156px past
  its own card (`838..1377`). Its sibling `input.input` sits at the same right edge, so the control
  and its field are both off-screen.
- **Why it is unreachable, not merely wide:** the third grid column starts at ~1313 and its content
  is 220px wide; no ancestor scrolls horizontally; the nearest clipping ancestor is
  `main.main-content` with `overflow-x: hidden`.
- **Reproducible:** two fresh navigations, same rect. Reproduces with the FFmpeg prompt open and
  after dismissing it.
- **Not caused by this session's X-06 work:** hiding the new `.guidance-reusable` field in the live
  DOM leaves the rect identical (`1533` → `1533`), and the session's diff only mentions
  `avoid_legible_text` inside payload/preset hunks, not this markup.

### F-15b — Scene Review: the command bar runs 13px off-screen at 1440×900
- **Element:** `div.region.generate-region` (the primary call to action plus status) inside
  `div.top-action-bar` → `div.review-command-bar` → `div.review-workspace` → `main.main-content`.
- **Geometry:** rect `1150..1453` — **13px past the viewport**, clipped. Also carries
  `span.popover-root`, `button.btn.btn-primary.btn-primary-cta`
  ("Finish 0 missing images · may use credits") and `p.generate-status` ("Pick an episode first").
- **Reproducible** on a fresh navigation; no horizontal scroller in the chain.
