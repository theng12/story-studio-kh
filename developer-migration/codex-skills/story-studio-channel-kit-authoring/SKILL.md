# Story Studio Channel Kit Legacy Compatibility

Channel Kits are retired from new authoring and merchandising. This skill exists only for legacy compatibility and safe routing.

- For any new channel concept, style, thumbnail, formula, topic, production option, or Content Store product, use `$story-studio-content-pack-authoring`. Create independently purchasable, mix-and-matchable packs only.
- Do not create, extend, reserve, build, price, stock, or publish a Channel Kit. Do not add `bundle_id`, coordinator products, Kit dependencies, or Kit merchandising to a new handoff.
- Existing Channel Kits remain readable and installable for customers who already use them. You may inspect their exact closure, validate historical archives, or diagnose compatibility without changing the Kit.
- A request to “make a kit” is not an exception. Translate it into the requested standalone component packs unless the owner explicitly changes the global no-Kit policy first.

Do not copy the legacy Kit's bundle structure into new products. Preserve its records and customer access while keeping all new work on the standalone Content Pack contract.
