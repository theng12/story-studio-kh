# Motivation

**Resolved perspective:** Direct second-person coach, formula-managed.

**Quality contract:** Earned conviction, not hype. Name the cost, trade-off, and next concrete action.

**Opening and outline:** Start with a real avoidance, decision, or cost. Develop one concrete idea per minute with a specific example or practice.

**Avoid:** “Rise and grind,” destiny language, guaranteed outcomes, shouting typography translated into prose, and an escalating hype crescendo.
