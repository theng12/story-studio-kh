# Paid Content Store wave boundaries

This document is an admission policy for later paid Content Store waves. The lanes below are
approved directions for future proposals, not stocked products, product IDs, prices, releases,
or permission to repackage an unchanged legacy file.

## Approved expansion lanes

The free foundation names the customer direction. The paid depth path must add material value
to that direction; the path is not a promise that any product will be published.

| Free foundation | Approved paid depth path |
| --- | --- |
| Wild Worlds | Enriched Fungi, Insects & Arthropods; Plants & Botanical Survival; then a Natural World specialist collection |
| First Humans | Enriched Environments & Climate; Social Life & Belief; Tools & Technology; then a complete Ancient Humans collection |
| Deep Time | Enriched Prehistoric Life & Extinction; Ancient Oceans & Deep Sea; Human Origins & Ancient Worlds; Speculative Earth Scenarios |
| Business Explained | Enriched Ancient Civilizations & Empires; industries; finance; and company-specific expansions |
| Human Behavior | Authored relationships, persuasion, group behavior, boundaries, and decision-making expansions |
| History Explained | Archival Ledger and later evidence-led specialist systems |
| A Life As… | Enriched Criminal Hierarchies and Law Enforcement Hierarchies, plus future occupation systems |
| Calm Mind | Additional practice settings and specialist calm programs that avoid medical or therapeutic claims |
| Build Momentum | Additional habit, planning, recovery, and constraint-based action packs |
| Sleep Journeys | Additional settings, narrative structures, and premium visual/thumbnail looks that preserve low-arousal behavior |

A lane is not a catalog entry. Each proposed wave must identify its own scope, evidence, and
compatibility before authoring starts.

## Net-new outcome

The proposal and eventual detail page must name a customer outcome that an unchanged free or
legacy-included component does not already deliver. “More of the same,” a renamed bundle, or a
new cover is not a net-new outcome. The outcome must be observable in the supported workflow,
such as a new editorial direction, a materially broader structured planning space, or a new
production look that changes the usable result.

## Material content

A paid wave must add material structured coverage, introduce a distinct Formula contract, or
provide an original production look. Structured coverage means authored dimensions, subjects,
mechanisms, evidence lenses, recipes, or equivalent declared content that the runtime can use;
cosmetic copy, title paraphrases, relabelled legacy rows, and bundle-only rearrangement do not
qualify. The source and its acceptance tests must make the difference inspectable and
repeatable.

## No legacy rebilling

Unchanged legacy content and files cannot be sold or rebilled as a new paid wave. Existing
projects retain their current access and pins, and legacy built-ins remain usable while a new
wave is evaluated. A replacement or migration may be offered only as an explicit, separately
reviewed choice with new content and a new release; authoring a paid wrapper never withdraws,
rewrites, or silently recharges unchanged bytes.

## Independent identity

When a component needs independent ownership, updates, withdrawal, or review, the proposal
must define it as a separate catalog identity and entitlement family. A kit is a composition of
exact component releases, not a disguised monolith. GenStudio assigns the eventual product,
release, SKU, and family identities after approval; this policy invents none, and Story Studio
must not guess or cache them.

## Proofable claim

Every numerical, coverage, capability, compatibility, or completeness claim must be derived
from the signed release and backed by a named offline acceptance test. Claims are not inferred
from a title, a folder count, a legacy file, or an authoring intention. The proposal records the
source of each claim, and an independent reviewer may reject the wave when the evidence cannot
be reproduced from the exact release under review.

## Complete use

No component may be sold as an unusable orphan. A standalone Topic, Style, or Recipe must work
with at least one compatible free or already-owned Formula; a specialist Formula must declare
its compatible content; and a kit must include or name every component required for its promised
workflow. The acceptance test must exercise the supported use path, not merely validate that a
manifest exists.

## Transparent dependency

The detail view and quote must name every required component and state whether it is free,
already owned, or included. The customer sees the exact GenStudio quote and ownership facts;
Story Studio does not calculate a price, discount, grant, or dependency value locally. Missing
or unresolved dependency facts block admission until the proposal is corrected.

## Wave admission and publication

1. **Proposal before authoring.** A written proposal must be accepted against all seven gates
   above before paid-wave authoring or commercial identity assignment begins.
2. **Evidence before merchandising.** The authored source is validated, its claims are tested,
   and its dependencies are reviewed before any shelf or detail metadata is prepared.
3. **Owner price after GenStudio recommendation.** GenStudio recommends a price from the live
   credit economy; the owner makes the final price decision and approves it only after that
   recommendation and the evidence review. No later wave price is guessed in Story Studio.
4. **Independent wave review.** Each wave requires an independent wave review covering content
   distinctness, legacy boundaries, claims, identity, dependencies, and commercial wording.
   Critical or Important findings block the wave until corrected and re-reviewed.
5. **Explicit publication.** No automatic publication, grant, purchase, installation, or shelf
   placement follows from a passing test. Publication requires the separate owner-approved
   release and GenStudio catalog actions; without those actions the wave remains proposal or
   source material only.

This policy creates no new product ID, SKU, release ID, entitlement family, price, discount, or
catalog listing. It is a gate for future work, not a pre-sale or a release instruction.
