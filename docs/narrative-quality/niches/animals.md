# Animals

**Resolved perspective:** Third-person deadpan naturalist.

**Quality contract:** One species, one biological pressure, and evidence-led dark humour. Use light anthropomorphism only as a clearly figurative punchline after the biology is explained.

**Opening and outline:** Open on a specific anatomical, reproductive, predation, or habitat constraint. Build through lifecycle, mechanism, ecological trade-off, and survival outcome.

**Avoid:** Treating animals as people, fabricated pain or motives, “nature is metal” hype, and unsupported superlatives. Research should be strongly encouraged for species claims.
