## Customer outcome

Archival Ledger is for a customer who wants to publish measured, long-form historical investigations without rebuilding a channel format for every episode. It turns familiar historical outcomes into causal reconstructions: institutions, finance, logistics, trade, political choices, material constraints, and the people who bore their costs.

The pack should reliably carry a project through ideas, scripts, scene direction, and thumbnails. It does not promise finished videos, factual certainty without research, or a fixed number of generated episodes. “500+ ideas” means the included catalog and diversity rules support at least 500 materially distinct episode assignments before title wording is considered.

## Included production system

The signed bundle is distilled from the current Hidden Mechanics of History production system:

- One evidence-led historical-causality Series Formula.
- Five compatible historical Topic Packs, initially covering 125 named subjects:
  - states, revolutions, and collapse;
  - money, debt, and financial institutions;
  - empires, trade, and extraction;
  - wars, treaties, and world order;
  - infrastructure, commodities, and corporate power.
- The Archival Documentary Reconstruction visual style.
- The Archival Map Investigation thumbnail recipe.

These are implementation facts for the detail/receipt views. The shelf card sells the outcome, not “eight components.”

## Why the 500+ promise is defensible

The formula and Topic Packs do not treat title strings as uniqueness. Each assignment is distinguished by:

- named subject;
- bounded episode object such as an institution, policy, transaction, document, faction, infrastructure system, decision, or period;
- time and place;
- causal mechanism;
- causal stage;
- popular-account gap;
- evidence posture.

The launch catalog has 125 named subjects. Allowing up to four genuinely separate sibling episodes per subject supports 500 assignments, while the independent axes supply further breadth. Siblings are accepted only when their episode object and mechanism differ; title paraphrases remain duplicates.

Example siblings under one broad subject:

- how public debt changed state capacity;
- how tax collection shifted the political coalition;
- how a liquidity crisis reached ordinary households;
- how an international settlement constrained later choices.

## Pricing and operator discount ruling

The owner-approved list price is **2,000 GenStudio credits** for the complete opening bundle.
The approved launch offer is a plain operator-set **90% discount for every customer**, with an
optional end date of **2026-12-31** for this offer. It is not a new-customer promotion:
there is no eligibility rule, account-history evaluation, first-purchase semantics, or
discount-refusal state.

GenStudio calculates the quote by deducting the exact value of owned components first, then
applying the active 90% operator discount to the missing-component remainder. The final amount
and discount facts are signed by GenStudio. Story Studio renders those facts and never
calculates the list price, component deduction, discount, final amount, or eligibility locally.
The final wire field names remain a GenStudio contract input and must not be guessed in this
proposal.

## Release facts to prepare after approval

- stable pack ID and entitlement family;
- semantic version `1.0.0`;
- minimum compatible Story Studio version;
- signed `.sspack` archive no larger than Story Studio's 50 MiB safety ceiling;
- SHA-256 digest and exact byte size;
- component summary and one-line changelog;
- a self-contained first release with no external pack dependencies;
- GenStudio product title, summary, description, cover assets, and showcase entries.

The first release must remain self-contained because GenStudio 1.6 restore currently publishes only each owned family's current release. Story cannot safely retrieve an older exact dependency after that family advances. External dependencies become eligible only after GenStudio publishes signed, account-authorized historical release lookup/download metadata.

The production publisher key is a separate trust gate: Story Studio must ship the stable public key, while the matching private key is generated and retained outside the repository and outside application bundles. The existing free-recovery test key is not a paid-pack production key.
