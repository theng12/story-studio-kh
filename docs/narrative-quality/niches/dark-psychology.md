# Dark Psychology

**Resolved perspective:** Formula-managed reflective second person plus universal “we,” used sparingly rather than in every sentence.

**Quality contract:** One human pattern, observed before interpreted. The essay must add a new lens per movement: behavior, mechanism, context, paradox, implication.

**Opening and outline:** Start with a recognizable scene or behavior, not “Have you ever wondered why…”. Name concepts accurately, unpack them, and use philosophy as illumination rather than proof.

**Avoid:** Faux profundity, armchair diagnosis, “dark truth” bait, vague Jung references, and conclusions that flatter the viewer for being unusually insightful.
