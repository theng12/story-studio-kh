# Story Studio Ten-Recipe Thumbnail Expansion Design

**Status:** Owner-approved concept portfolio; detailed design awaiting owner review before implementation planning

**Date:** 2026-08-24

**Scope:** Ten standalone Story Studio `thumbnail-recipe` Content Pack proposals, their composition contracts, preview matrices, qualification gates, and later Store reachability. This document creates no registry identity, source pack, raster, archive, product, release, price, Kit mutation, generation job, or publication.

**Builds on:** `2026-08-23-modular-content-expansion-portfolio-design.md`, the deployed strict `thumbnail-recipe` schema, `content-packs/authoring-registry.json`, and the Story Studio thumbnail-adoption workflow.

## 1. Owner decision and outcome

The owner approved all ten researched thumbnail systems as an expansion portfolio:

1. Before/After Transformation
2. Two-Sided Comparison
3. Reaction + Evidence Inset
4. Cutaway / Exploded View
5. Hero + Payoff Object
6. Iconic Result Close-Up
7. Scale Journey
8. Numbered Lineup
9. Macro Mystery Detail
10. Hands-On POV Moment

Each becomes one independently versioned Thumbnail Recipe proposal. The three previews required for each recipe are customer-facing range examples, not an A/B-testing feature or three sub-recipes. A user chooses a Recipe; its gallery demonstrates that the same composition grammar supports one-, two-, and three-line titles plus materially different subjects, angles, distances, settings, and visual styles.

This portfolio requires no new application schema or creative kind. It uses the current `thumbnail-recipe` document and existing Content Pack delivery path.

## 2. Fixed boundaries

- A Recipe owns 16:9 composition, focal hierarchy, title-safe space, headline behavior, and recipe-specific exclusions.
- The active Visual Style remains the final authority for medium, palette, texture, lighting, and rendering technique.
- Formula and Topic Pack remain the authorities for editorial promise, episode structure, subject coverage, evidence, and safety.
- Every image is a single native full-frame render. The image model renders the complete intended headline inside the image.
- No post-render title overlay, title plate, masking, inpainting, compositing, collage assembly, or UI screenshot is permitted.
- The ten proposals are standalone component releases, not a Channel Kit and not ten kit-specific copies.
- Component products may be stocked as draft/non-purchasable. Customer reachability occurs only through a separately qualified active Channel Kit that pins an exact recipe release.
- This control/development Mac may author, validate, inspect, checksum, test, and coordinate. It never acts as a fleet worker and never executes image generation.

## 3. Research-backed product rules

YouTube recommends custom thumbnails that are accurate, readable, compositionally clear, and not overly complex. Its current native testing surface accepts up to three title/thumbnail choices and recommends meaningfully different concepts instead of cosmetic variants. Story Studio uses the same useful cardinality—three previews—but does not require or implement A/B testing.

Official references:

- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9229980

Every Recipe preview gallery therefore contains exactly three original `1672x941` WebPs:

- one preview with an exact one-line headline;
- one preview with an exact two-line headline; and
- one preview with an exact three-line headline.

The three examples must be materially different across subject, side/angle/distance, setting, and palette/symbol while preserving the Recipe's defining composition invariant. A palette-only change, close duplicate, malformed title, generic hero layout, or existing Recipe restyle does not qualify.

## 5. Exact recipe contracts and preview matrices

The text below is the design brief, not an executable provider prompt. A later implementation plan converts each approved row into an immutable Studio Hub prompt batch only after registry reservation and receipt verification.

### 5.1 Before/After Transformation — `before-after-proof-panel`

**Grammar:** The same subject changes across a clear left-to-right temporal seam. The left state establishes a truthful baseline; the right state shows a credible observed outcome. Reserve one upper-center title-safe band. Do not fabricate labels, measurements, causality, or transformation claims.

| Lines | Exact sample headline | Preview brief |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | `FROM SEED TO SPROUT` | Side view of one seed tray: bare soil left becoming a healthy small sprout right; greenhouse setting; earth-to-green palette. |
| 2 | `A DULL ROOM` / `BECOMES CALM` | Wide frontal view of the same modest room, cluttered/dim left and tidy/daylit right; slate-to-sand-and-sage palette. |
| 3 | `FROM ROUGH` / `TO READY` / `TO USE` | Three-quarter tabletop view of one unfinished wooden stool becoming a finished usable stool; simple workshop; raw-to-honey wood. |

### 5.2 Two-Sided Comparison — `two-option-criteria-split`

**Grammar:** Two equivalent present-time alternatives face each other across a central divide with equal visual weight. Reserve one upper-center title band. Do not imply chronology, hide criteria, preselect a winner, or present a universal recommendation.

| Lines | Exact sample headline | Preview brief |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | `PAPER OR DIGITAL?` | Symmetrical overhead desk with a handwritten planning sheet left and an unbranded e-ink-like planning device right; no recognizable UI. |
| 2 | `QUIET SPACE` / `OR BUSY SPACE?` | Eye-level split interior using the same chair silhouette in a minimal reading nook and a lively object-dense nook. |
| 3 | `LIGHT OR` / `DARK FOR` / `FOCUS?` | Two desk-lamp conditions viewed at the same angle, bright task light left and low amber task light right; neutral center cue. |

### 5.3 Reaction + Evidence Inset — `reaction-evidence-inset`

**Grammar:** One generic or rights-cleared reaction subject dominates one side. One rounded evidence window is generated natively inside the same scene on the other side and is connected through gaze or gesture. It must contain an original object/detail, never copied UI, private data, or a copyrighted screenshot.

| Lines | Exact sample headline | Preview brief |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | `THAT'S THE CLUE` | Generic field researcher in profile looking toward a native circular detail of a leaf-vein pattern; outdoor trail; forest green and parchment. |
| 2 | `THE TINY DETAIL` / `CHANGES EVERYTHING` | Generic baker reacting toward a native close-detail window showing properly risen dough texture; warm cream and terracotta kitchen. |
| 3 | `ONE SMALL` / `SIGN TELLS` / `THE STORY` | Generic mechanic gesturing toward a native close-detail window of one worn bicycle chain link; charcoal and yellow workshop. |

### 5.4 Cutaway / Exploded View — `cutaway-exploded-object`

**Grammar:** One central object exposes a small, meaningful set of internal layers or components in ordered depth. Reserve upper-left title space. Do not invent safety-critical internals, overload the frame with labels, or turn it into a surrounding callout board.

| Lines | Exact sample headline | Preview brief |
| ---: | --- | --- |

| 2 | `HOW A THERMOS` / `KEEPS HEAT` | Angled cutaway of an unbranded thermos showing outer wall, vacuum gap, inner wall, and warm liquid; midnight blue and copper. |
| 3 | `THE LAYERS` / `THAT MAKE` / `A ROOF WORK` | Isometric cutaway of a small generic roof section with shingles, membrane, insulation, and rafters; no construction guarantee. |

Reserve an upper-center title band. Do not use exaggerated outcomes, branded products, creator likenesses, or a lone object without a host.

**Grammar:** Three to five equal-status objects, people, or examples appear in a precise row or grid, each with one large native numeral. The count, numerals, and visible entries must agree exactly. **Grammar:** One extreme close-up makes an ordinary subject briefly ambiguous while a small contextual clue at the frame edge preserves honesty. Reserve one lower-left title-safe field. Do not add a reaction host, separate inset, false reveal, or unsupported mystery claim.

## 7. Authoring and preview-production wave

### 7.1 One atomic reservation

After owner approval of this written design, one coordinator validates the live registry and requests exactly ten `thumbnail-recipe` records in one compare-and-swap event. It uses the ten proposed slugs and no fallback identities. A collision, stale revision, registry lock, or returned mismatch fails the entire reservation. Each exact persisted record is verified against the single returned receipt before any source is authored.

### 7.2 One immutable thirty-image prompt batch

After receipt verification, Story writes one draft prompt batch containing exactly thirty text-only/no-reference-image jobs: three per recipe and one for each approved preview row. The prompt batch records exact target paths, titles, line counts, composition invariants, diversity axes, exclusions, evidence requirements, and batch SHA-256.

The batch is a quote input only. Studio Hub must return a field-exact capability response and quote. Generation remains blocked until the owner separately approves the exact batch hash, route/model/provider, unit cost, maximum credit spend, rights basis, capacity, and job authority. A zero-credit route still requires approval.

### 7.3 Returned evidence

Every returned image must include untouched original bytes, job/request ID, named worker, exact submitted prompt and hash, provider/model, timestamp, seed if published, cost receipt, rights/license basis, source/reference declaration, SHA-256, byte size, file type, dimensions, decode result, and exact-title transcription.

The control Mac verifies but does not repair creative defects. It may perform only a documented deterministic whole-image conversion when the return is not already the exact required WebP. It may not mask, composite, edit individual regions, replace text, or create a title plate.

## 8. Qualification and packaging

Each Recipe is independently eligible only when:

1. its registry receipt verifies;
2. all three returned rasters pass provenance, rights, decode, exact dimensions, exact-title, line-count, originality, and material-diversity checks;
3. its source manifest and `thumbnail-recipe.json` use only receipt-issued identity values and the approved grammar;
4. focused schema, runtime-adapter, compatibility, title-safety, and source-provenance tests pass;
5. independent schema, originality/provenance, compatibility, prompt-equivalence, visual/thumbnail, safety/copy, and commercial-boundary reviews pass;
6. two keyless `genstudio-store-receipt-v1` builds using the truthful resolved minimum app version are byte- and SHA-identical, validate, contain no executable/URL/credential/provider/model/price fields, and remain below 52,428,800 bytes; and
7. the coordinator performs a receipt-bound `reserved` to `authored` transition only after every applicable gate is clean.

Partial qualification is allowed. One failed Recipe remains reserved and quarantined without blocking the other nine from candidate delivery.

## 9. Store and customer reachability

The initial receiver handoff may request draft component products and published releases only after candidates validate. It does not activate standalone component products, set price/discount, purchase, grant, charge, deploy, tag, or publish a Channel Kit.

The owner's standing policy remains:

- component products stay draft and non-purchasable;
- customers receive components only through active Channel Kits;
- a Kit pins exact component versions and hashes;
- no Kit closure changes automatically when a newer Recipe release appears; and
- each Kit addition/successor is a separate reviewed and owner-authorized stocking action.

## 10. Planned implementation decomposition

The later implementation plan should use these tasks:

1. registry collision recheck and one ten-record reservation;
2. receipt verification plus thirty-job prompt-batch authoring;
3. Studio Hub quote gate and owner job-authority stop;
4. returned-evidence intake and quarantine review;
5. ten independent Recipe source/preview/provenance authoring tasks;
6. ten independent spec reviews and ten independent quality/visual reviews;
7. serialized registry transitions for clean candidates only;
8. deterministic double-build and external artifact ledger;
9. final whole-wave review and exact GenStudio draft-component handoff; and
10. separate later Kit reachability wave.

The implementation should exploit parallelism only after the single shared reservation and returned preview batch. No two workers may concurrently edit the registry, shared provenance ledger, shared tests, or external artifact ledger.

## 12. Design self-review

- **Scope:** Exactly ten standalone Thumbnail Recipe proposals; no new schema or Kit is smuggled into the wave.
- **Distinctness:** Every proposal has a positive invariant and explicit boundaries against the current/reserved catalog.
- **Preview completeness:** Thirty rows cover one-, two-, and three-line headlines with real composition diversity.
- **Truth and safety:** Transformations, comparisons, evidence, results, scale, counts, reveals, and actions are bounded to what the image/video can honestly support.
- **Identity discipline:** Slugs are proposals; authoritative identities come only from a future atomic reservation and verified receipt.
- **Rights:** All examples are original, generic, unbranded, no-reference-image briefs with no creator likeness or copied trade dress.
- **Operational boundary:** The control Mac never generates. Studio Hub cannot act before exact quote and later owner job authority.
- **Commerce boundary:** Candidate handoff and draft stocking do not create customer reachability. Active Kit changes remain separately reviewed and authorized.
- **Ambiguity:** The three previews are gallery choices/examples, not an A/B implementation or sub-recipe schema.
