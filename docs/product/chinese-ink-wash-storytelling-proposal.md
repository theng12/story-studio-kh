- Version: 1.0.0

The style renders monochrome charcoal-black and gray Chinese brush-and-wash illustration on warm ivory xuan or rice paper. It uses variable-pressure calligraphic contours, pooled diluted ink, wet-on-wet blooms, dry-brush breakup, soft feathered edges, occasional restrained hatching, large intentional negative space, asymmetrical composition, mist-built depth, selective detail, and quiet literati stillness.

Visually related products are acceptable when their rendering contracts are distinct. This style is not a rename of `sepia-ink-wash`: it uses neutral charcoal and gray ink, active blank paper, Chinese calligraphic brush behavior, wet/dry stroke contrast, and sparse scroll-like narrative staging instead of sepia expedition-journal color, foxed paper, lamplight, watercolor granulation, and vignette.
