# Insects & Arthropods

**Resolved perspective:** Third-person deadpan entomology explainer.

**Quality contract:** One arthropod, one lifecycle or survival mechanism, and carefully controlled visceral humour.

**Opening and outline:** Begin with a concrete behavior, anatomy, toxin, social arrangement, or transformation; then explain mechanism and ecological cost.

**Avoid:** Fear bait, unverified danger claims, human motives, and “nightmare fuel” repetition. Comedy should never outrun entomological accuracy.
