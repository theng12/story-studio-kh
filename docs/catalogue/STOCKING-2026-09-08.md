## Flagged, not fixed: the shelf label still says "Lost Voyages"

The rename reached the pack document — which is the name a customer sees in Story Studio's
library — but **not** the store product title. `stock_pack` reuses an existing product and
never edits it, and `ContentStoreService` has no route that renames a product: merchandising
updates cover media, tags and licence only. Changing it today would mean a raw SQL write
against a live product, which is precisely what the stocking runbook exists to avoid.

Two things follow, both needing the owner rather than a rerun:

1. The shopfront still advertises the channel name. If that is to change, the store needs a
   product-title update route (admin console + service method), and then a rerun renames it.
2. `stock_pack` warns loudly when a reused product's **kind**, **price** or **publisher**
   disagrees with the manifest, but says nothing when the **title** does — so this divergence
   was silent. That check is worth adding wherever the rename route lands.
