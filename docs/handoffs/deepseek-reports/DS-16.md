## X-05 — limitations record

These are **limits**, not reproduced defects. Each is stated with its reachability so a future reader
does not mistake a documented boundary for an open bug, and none is treated as licence for an
architecture change.

1. **Platform-conditional `/var` alias skip.** macOS reports `/var` as `/private/var`; the affected
   check is skipped where the platform has no such alias. Reachability: real on this Mac, but it is a
   platform property, not a defect — the skip is correct and should stay conditional.
2. **Final path-check/unlink race.** Node's path-based removal has a window between the last check and
   the unlink. It is narrowed by the app-level coordination lock and `O_NOFOLLOW`/exclusive-creation
   writes, and it cannot be closed by a path-based API. Testable only by injecting a fault at that exact
   syscall boundary; not worth a redesign without a repro.
3. **Unproven physical power-loss behaviour.** Durability is asserted by fsync points; actual behaviour
   under sudden power loss was never proven and cannot be proven on this Mac without fault-injecting
   hardware or a filesystem. Recorded as unproven.
4. **Multi-file crash transactions.** TTS activation is not a universal multi-file crash transaction: a
   crash between files can leave a partially promoted set. What compensates is the surrounding design —
   quarantine-before-reset, durable recovery incidents, and provider-evidence guards — and those are
   covered by tests. Recorded as a limit of the write model, not a pending repair.
