# Ancient Humans

**Formula role:** Long-form, evidence-aware survival and daily-life explainers. Topic Packs already cover survival, environments, social life, and technology.

**Resolved perspective:** Third-person documentary. The viewer should not be role-played into prehistory by default.

**Opening policy:** Start with a concrete pressure, object, action, or modern assumption that fails under prehistoric conditions. Never open with “Imagine you are a caveman” or a generic sensory invitation.

**Outline contract:** Environment and period, practical problem, available evidence, adaptation options, trade-off, variation across groups or eras, and a modest conclusion. Each chapter needs one specific material reality: hearth, water source, tool, animal, shelter, injury, season, or social practice.

**Quality guardrails:** Mark uncertainty honestly; do not flatten all ancient humans into one culture; avoid heroic fantasy, presentism, and invented interior thoughts. Prefer “the evidence suggests” only when evidence is actually attached, not as decorative hedging.

**Future formula work:** Add a 7–9 movement survival arc and a research-fact-to-outline-beat link. Existing “Curious survival storyteller” remains a voice, not an immersive second-person instruction.
