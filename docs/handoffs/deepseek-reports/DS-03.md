## Reproduction and root cause
- Exact fixture and expected versus actual:
  1. A disposable project gets an episode `EP0001` with a long narration chapter and **no** `audio_tts_model.json` record and **no** audio job of its own.
  2. `jobs.json` carries two terminal `audio` jobs for *other* episodes with **different** models, served by a fake live catalogue: `project-model-small` (`maximum_input_characters: 200` → effective 180) and `project-model-large` (200,000). The small-budget job is the most recent.
  3. `getAudioStatus(slug, 'EP0001')` resolves no selected model and no record, then reaches the legacy history borrow.
  4. Actual (pre-fix): `audioJobs.find(thisEpisode) || audioJobs[0]` picks the most recent job of **any** episode — `project-model-small` — and predicts 12 `EP0001_Chapter_01_partN.mp3` files that this episode was never meant to have.
  - Expected per AGENTS.md §4: another episode's model must not determine this episode's split. With conflicting project history there is no derivable value, so the section is expected whole — the same honest answer the no-history case already returns.
- RED command, exit, failing assertion; why this is not a setup failure:
  - `node scripts/testing/run-backend-tests.mjs --only audio-tts-model-record` → **exit 1**; tests 7, pass 6, fail 1. The three S-04 cases are in the file; only the cross-model one failed.
  - Failing assertion (verbatim, abridged): `a conflicting project history must not decide this episode's split; got ["EP0001_Chapter_01_part1.mp3" … "EP0001_Chapter_01_part12.mp3"]`.
  - Not a setup failure: the same run's controls passed — this episode's own job wins even when another episode is newer, and a single project-wide model is still borrowed. The fake catalogue was proven live because the small-budget model genuinely produced 12 parts (the split only happens when the published budget is read).
- Current-source callers and ownership boundary:
  - `getAudioStatus` (`audio-reconciler.ts:215`) is the status/readiness planner; its `expected` filenames drive audio readiness, transcription gating and the Audio page.
  - Resolution precedence: caller's live selection → this episode's `audio_tts_model.json` record (`readAudioTtsModelRecord`) → legacy job-history borrow. Only the last step was contaminated.
  - `activeAudioSources` (`:132`) additionally needs the resolved `model` to validate the active selection and provenance; a wrong borrowed model made a valid multi-part selection look invalid and dropped the provenance map.
