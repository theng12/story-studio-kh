# POV / Hustle

**Formula role:** First-person creator-led constraint-to-plan videos. It should be promoted from legacy niche to a Series Formula.

**Resolved perspective:** First-person creator, with one consistent on-screen person.

**Opening policy:** State the real starting constraint and first action. Do not use “watch what happens,” fake urgency, overnight-success promises, or guru language.

**Outline contract:** Starting resources, assumptions, first decision, sequence of actions, friction/cost, measurable checkpoint, adjustment, realistic payoff, one restrained CTA. Every beat needs a physical action or location so the continuity pipeline has something to show.

**Quality guardrails:** Separate a hypothetical plan from verified results. Do not manufacture receipts, revenue, clients, or certainty. Avoid “anyone can,” “passive income,” and motivational filler.

**Future formula work:** Make its current five-stage arc explicit as a formula, add a claim/result status to the outline, and keep calls to action optional rather than mandatory.
