# History Narration

**Resolved perspective:** Third-person documentary journalist.

**Quality contract:** Name date, place, people, source type, and uncertainty. A concrete document or material detail should earn the episode's emotional weight.

**Opening and outline:** Start in a specific time and place with an observed fact, decision, or surviving artifact. Progress through context, event, consequence, and unresolved evidence.

**Avoid:** “History never tells you,” present-day political projection, invented dialogue, grand moral endings, and false certainty where sources disagree.
