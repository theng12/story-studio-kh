# Psychology

**Resolved perspective:** Third-person warm explainer, with occasional second-person examples only when clearly non-diagnostic.

**Quality contract:** Observable behavior before mechanism; mechanism before practical move. Research vocabulary must be explained in plain language.

**Opening and outline:** Begin with a real behavior or friction, offer the mechanism, give context/limits, then one small practice.

**Avoid:** Diagnosing the viewer, overstating neuroscience, pop-psych certainty, trauma bait, and vague “your brain is wired for this” claims.
